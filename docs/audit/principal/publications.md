# Principal: Publications (/publications)

## 1. Who and why
The Principal (and the research cell, Finance) when she needs to find a claim, or see everything the scheme has handled for one department or one journal. It is a lookup, not a report: "where does this paper stand, and what was it worth?" Weekly at most.

## 2. What it showed today
Screenshot: `shots/publications-before.png`.

- "Every publication across the college that this account may see, queried directly." Two jargon words ("queried directly") and a promise the page cannot keep: it lists 95 *claims*, not the college's 8,466 papers. A Principal counting "publications" from it would report 95.
- Claim numbers such as `ERP-PROCESSED-890` with no explanation. They are claims imported from the old ERP spreadsheet.
- Paid claims with the amount "Not recorded": a real gap in the imported data (paid through the ledger, no amount on the claim), said in small text with no hint of why.
- Blank cells drawn as "—" for a missing journal, year or quartile.
- "Export" did not say what it exports; "95 results · ₹2,43,355 total" did not say the total was claims' amounts.
- Nothing pointed to where the college's papers are.

## 3. What changes
- The subtitle says what the list is (every claim filed under the incentive scheme, and where it stands) and links to Papers (/reports/papers) for the papers the college has published.
- Old-ERP claim numbers read "Old ERP, PROCESSED-890", with the full explanation on hover; a claim with no number says "No claim number".
- Missing journal, year and quartile are the kit's "Not recorded"; a card on a phone says which three are missing.
- "Download these 95 claims" says what the file holds; the count reads "95 claims".
- Filters, sorting and the stacked phone cards are unchanged.
