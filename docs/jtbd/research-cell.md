# Jobs to be done: Research cell and Research coordinator

Two people sit at the first desk of the chain (Faculty files, **Research cell
clears**, Principal approves, Director authorises, Finance pays). The Research
cell (research supervisor) checks each claim against the record. The Research
coordinator owns two scheme rules: research-faculty quotas and final-year
project (FYP) claims.

Grounding (retrieved 2026-09-28): Indian incentive policies pay only on
indexed venues and ask the office to check the index itself, not the
publisher's site ([KIET incentive policy](https://www.old.kiet.edu/incentive-for-researchers),
[SDUAHER incentive policy](https://sduaher.ac.in/storage/1746438393.0445IncentivepolicywithAddednudm.1tR7.pdf),
[CASRAI checklist](https://casrai.org/guides/how-to-identify-predatory-journals-and-publishers)).
Journals get discontinued from Scopus and cloned titles reuse ISSNs, a known
Indian problem ([Predatory publishing in Scopus, QSS 2022](https://direct.mit.edu/qss/article/3/3/859/113621/Predatory-publishing-in-Scopus-Evidence-on-cross)).
Search terms used: "university research office verify publication incentive
claim Scopus indexed affiliation quartile predatory journal policy India".

Each job: the question they ask, the decision it drives, the artefact that
proves it.

| # | Job | Question | Decision | Artefact |
|---|-----|----------|----------|----------|
| 1 | Check indexing | Is this journal in Scopus/WoS for the year the paper came out? | Clear or send back | Record vs claim table, Scopus/Scimago source line |
| 2 | Check quartile | Is the quartile the faculty wrote the one the record gives for that year? | Pay on the record quartile, or reject an inflated one | "Claimed Q1, record Q3" line |
| 3 | Check affiliation | Does the byline carry the college's name? | No affiliation, no payment | Affiliation state on the ticket |
| 4 | Check author position | Is the claimant where they say in the author list, and how many authors? | Author share of the amount | Position and count from the record |
| 5 | Watch doubtful venues | Is the journal discontinued, off UGC-CARE, or on our own watch-list? | Hold or reject; add to the watch-list | Journal standing and the college watch-list with a reason |
| 6 | Stop double payment | Has this DOI been paid, or claimed by a co-author here? | Reject the duplicate, pay one | Duplicates page, ledger match on the ticket |
| 7 | Keep turnaround short | What has waited longest, and how long? | Order of work, chase the stuck | Queue oldest first, ageing buckets |
| 8 | Process in batches | Which clean tickets can be cleared together? | Bulk clear | Bulk clear with a per-ticket result |
| 9 | Report the month | How many came in, were cleared, sent back, rejected; how fast? | What to tell the Principal | Monthly processing report and CSV |
| 10 | Answer faculty | Why was my paper sent back; when will it move? | Reply once, in writing | Messages from faculty, ticket history |
| 11 | Keep the database for NAAC | Is every cleared paper in the publication record? | Fix gaps before the audit | Publications, Imports, Archive, CSV downloads |
| 12 | Apply the research quota (coordinator) | Is this paper inside the member's first N unpaid papers this year? | Pay nothing, or pay the surplus | Quota N on People, "paper k of N" on the ticket |
| 13 | Apply the FYP rule (coordinator) | One claim per team, by the mentor, Rs 15,000 per conference? | Refuse a second claim or a non-mentor filer | Team, mentor and scheme note on the ticket |
| 14 | Never judge your own paper | Is this my claim? | Someone else clears it | Own claims left out of the queue, refused by the server |
