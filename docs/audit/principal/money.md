# Principal: Money hub (/money)

## 1. Who and why
The Principal before she approves a batch, and before the council. Question Q5 of docs/jtbd/principal.md: "How much of this year's money is gone, and is there room?" The pages behind the hub are Budget (the money position), Monthly statements and Policy.

## 2. What it showed today
Screenshot: `shots/money-before.png`.

- Three rows named for pages (Budget, Monthly statements, Policy) and nothing else: no number, no answer. The Principal had to open Budget to learn whether the year had a budget at all.
- The finding she needed was one page away: no budget is set for FY 2026-27, so approving claims has nothing to be weighed against.
- Two columns for three rows.

## 3. What changes
- The answer strip: the budget for the year (or "Not set", said in words with what it means: "nothing to weigh spending against"), paid so far this year, approved and waiting to be paid, and what is left (or "over the budget") when a budget exists. Each is a link to Budget.
- The same three pages below with what each is for. Other roles keep the generic hub.
- Not done: Budget (`/budget`) belongs to the office's money work and was not rebuilt; setting the budget is not the Principal's to do.
