# Social and discovery: jobs to be done

Who: every faculty member, and every officer (HOD, Principal, Director, research
cell, Finance) when they act as a colleague rather than as a desk. Nothing here
shows money.

| # | Job | Question they ask | Action in the app | Outcome they need |
|---|-----|-------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| 1 | Find a co-author for a specific paper idea | "Who here, or who we already know outside, has worked on ultrasound triage?" | Search (or Ctrl-K) a topic; Who to work with; Research scout; open the connection path to a person | A named colleague with a face, their papers on the topic, how I am connected to them, and a Message button that opens a conversation about the idea |
| 2 | Find a venue | "Where do people like me publish, and how fast?" | Discover (venues tab), Research scout, ask in Discussions | Journals my colleagues use, with quartile, and a thread where someone answers from experience |
| 3 | Get noticed for my work | "Does anyone see my paper?" | Share a paper to the feed; profile; leaderboard; wall of fame | The post shows the paper card; reactions and comments arrive as notifications; my rank and badges agree with my paper count |
| 4 | Ask a colleague a question | "Can I use your dataset?" | Message from their profile, or mention them in a post | They get one notification that opens the exact message or post; their reply shows up for me with an unread count |
| 5 | Celebrate a colleague | "Deepa got her first Q1, I want to say well done" | Congrats reaction, comment, or a post with a mention | The colleague is told who congratulated them and can open the post |
| 6 | Know what my department is working on | "What did CSE publish this term, who is working on what?" | Feed filtered to my department; Discover; leaderboard filtered by department; circle map | A department view with faces and paper titles, counts that match the leaderboard and the profile |

## What "done" looks like for every page in scope
- Faces on every person (photo, or initials when there is none).
- No raw markup: mention codes such as `@"Name"` render as a linked name.
- Every notification opens the thing it is about (the post, the message, the
  profile), not a list.
- Counts agree across Search, profile, leaderboard and the circle map.
- Works at 1280 and 390 px with no sideways scroll.
