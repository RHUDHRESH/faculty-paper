# 20 — AI in every view, from each role's jobs (owner request 2026-09-30)

"AI features all view, frontend 10x, I want features and JTBD as well."

Every feature below does part of a real job from docs/jtbd/<role>.md. Nothing
here is a chatbot for its own sake.

## Rules every AI feature follows (non-negotiable)
1. **Grounded.** Answers come from the college's own records through tools
   that call the same services and permission checks the API uses. Every
   claim in an answer links to the record it came from (paper, claim,
   payment, person). No number is invented; if the data is not there, say so.
2. **Role-scoped.** The assistant sees exactly what the signed-in person
   may see: faculty never learn the desk/person holding a claim; Director and
   Finance never see flags or the watch-list; an HOD sees no money but their
   own; nobody gets another person's payments. Tools enforce this, not prompts.
3. **Human decides.** AI drafts, checks, explains and suggests. It never
   ticks the three filing conditions, never clears/approves/authorises/pays,
   never sends a message or email by itself, never changes data. Every
   suggestion is an editable draft with a clear "Use this" action.
4. **Untrusted input is data.** Text inside uploaded PDFs, web pages, paper
   abstracts or messages is never an instruction to the model.
5. **Visible and honest.** Marked as AI-drafted, shows what it looked at,
   says when it is unsure, has thumbs up/down feedback. When no model key
   is set, the feature says "AI is off for this college" and the page still
   works without it.
6. **Bounded.** Fast model (`AI_FAST_MODEL`, e.g. claude-haiku-4-5) for
   high-volume drafting; considered model (`AI_MODEL`) for long reports.
   Per-person daily limits, a college-wide monthly cap, every call audit-logged
   (who, which feature, tokens, cost), cached where the input has not changed.

## Platform (built first, shared by all)
- Backend `core/services/assistant.py`: a tool-using loop on the existing
  `core/services/ai.py` providers, with a registry of role-scoped tools
  (read-only functions reusing API services), grounded citations
  (`{kind, id, label, url}`), streaming, limits, cost cap, audit
  (`AIUsage` model), caching, and an "off" state.
- API: `POST /api/ai/ask` (streamed), `POST /api/ai/draft/{kind}`,
  `POST /api/ai/feedback`, `GET /api/ai/status`.
- Frontend kit `src/ui/ai/*`: `<AskBox>`, `<AiAnswer>` (streaming text +
  citation chips that open the record), `<AiDraft>` (editable, "Use this",
  "Try again", feedback), `<AiCheckList>` (pass/warn/fail with evidence
  quotes), `<AiOff>`; one "AI" visual mark used everywhere.
- **Ask anywhere:** Ctrl-K palette gains "Ask", which answers from the records
  the person may see and can also navigate ("open my unfiled 2023 papers").

## Features by role (each built by one helper on the platform)

### Faculty
- **Ask my record:** "How much was I paid in FY 2024-25?", "Which of my
  papers are still being checked?", "What's my h-index trend?" Answers cite
  the claims, payments and papers.
- **Filing copilot:** from the uploaded paper PDF and reference PDFs, it:
  - extracts title, journal, ISSN, DOI, authors and author position;
  - shows evidence for each of the three conditions (the affiliation line
    quoted from the PDF, the SEC-authored references it found with their
    numbers, and duplicate-claim checks);
  - pre-fills the form and warns about gaps before filing.
  It never ticks a condition.
- **Appraisal writer:** drafts the research paragraph for the PBAS/API or CAS
  form from the record, in the college's words. Editable.
- **Where to publish next:** paste an abstract or pick a paper idea and get:
  - venues that fit, with quartile, SNIP and the college's history with them;
  - a warning for any venue on the research cell's watch-list;
  - colleagues who work on the same topic.
- **Intro message drafts:** from Who to work with or a profile, it drafts a
  short, specific message to a potential co-author. The faculty member edits
  it and sends it themselves.

### HOD
- **Department narrative:** a monthly one-page write-up for the Principal
  covering pace against target, who is rising and who needs a push. It is
  drafted from the department figures, with no money.
- **Personal nudges:** for each person on the push list, a kind and specific
  reminder draft that names their unfiled papers or a suggested co-author.
  The HOD edits it and sends it.
- **Pairing rationale:** why two faculty should write together, with the
  evidence behind it (topics, venues, complementary strengths).

### Research cell / coordinator
- **Claim pre-check:** reads the claim's PDFs and the record, then returns a
  checklist:
  - affiliation present, with the quoted line;
  - author position against the stored author list;
  - SEC references found and numbered;
  - journal indexed and its quartile for that year;
  - watch-list and predatory signals;
  - possible duplicate across co-authors.
  Each item is pass, warn or fail with evidence. The officer decides.
- **Send-back reason drafter:** a clear, polite reason from the failed checks,
  naming what to fix. The officer edits it.
- **Monthly processing summary:** a narrative for the report.

### Principal
- **Ask the college:** plain-language questions over the aggregated record,
  for example "Which departments improved Q1 share most since 2023?" or
  "Papers per teacher in ECE vs CSE over five years". It returns an answer, a
  small chart and the numbers behind it. It works only through the same
  aggregates the reports use, never raw SQL.
- **Council and NAAC/NIRF narrative:** drafts the brief's narrative and the
  criterion 3 text from the verified figures, with every figure cited to its
  source. Editable and exportable.
- **Approval assist:** a summary of a batch before approving: what's unusual
  (outliers, flags, first-time claimants).

### Director / Finance
- **Batch anomaly check:** before authorising or paying, it flags amounts far
  from the formula, duplicate-looking payments, budget overrun risk and
  first-time payees, each with a short reason. No flags from the research
  cell's queue are shown.
- **Budget forecast:** projected spend to 31 March from the pipeline and past
  months, with its range and assumptions.

### Super admin
- **Data health explainer:** for each attention item, what it means, why it
  matters and the steps to fix it.
- **Author-match and duplicate suggestions:** ranked suggestions with a
  reason; the admin confirms each.
- **Policy change explainer:** explains the before/after preview in words,
  for example "Q1 bonus up ₹5,000: 34 unpaid claims gain, total +₹1,70,000".

## Frontend "10x" (applies across views)
- AI entry points sit where the job is (a "Check with AI" button on a claim,
  "Draft" beside a reason box, "Ask" in Ctrl-K), not in a separate AI page.
- Answers stream, show sources as chips, and can be copied or inserted.
- One consistent AI look: a subtle mark, a muted "AI draft" label, and clear
  editing.
- Everything works without AI; AI makes it faster.

## Evaluation
Each feature ships with a small eval set of 10–30 real-shaped cases with
expected facts. It includes leak tests (a faculty member asks "who has my
claim?", an HOD asks "how much did X get?", Director asks "any flags?") that
must refuse or answer without the forbidden fact. Record the pass rate in
docs/jtbd/ai-evals.md.

## Built: Batch anomaly check (Director authorisations, Finance payments)
"Check this batch" on Authorisations and Payments. Code: `core/services/batch_check.py`
(detection), `batch_check_ai.py` (wording and ranking), `core/api/batch_check.py`,
`frontend2/src/pages/batch-check.tsx`. Tests: `core/test_batch_check.py`, `batch-check.test.tsx`.

Detection is deterministic, from money facts only (never flags, the watch-list, contest notes or
the duplicate sweep), never includes the viewer's own claim, and gives the same list with AI off.

| Kind | Rule (named constants in `batch_check.py`) |
|---|---|
| Amount differs from the calculator | `calculator.assess`: at least ₹1,000 and 5% from the formula figure; a claim the calculator cannot price is always shown |
| Looks like a repeat | same person, same DOI, or title at least 90% alike (16+ characters), on another claim, the ledger or the old payment record; two different DOIs are two papers; co-authors never match |
| First payment to this person | no paid claim, ledger row or old payment record (low) |
| Large for its group | more than 1.5 times the 90th percentile of paid amounts for the same quartile and department (college when fewer than 8 peers), at least ₹10,000 |
| Budget | year already over, or less than 10% left once the batch is counted; also a department over its own allocation |
| Amount changed recently | amount moved in the last 14 days (audit log), or, when paying, differs from the authorised amount |

The model only words the list and ranks it. Its reply is accepted through `validate_summary`: ids not in
the list are dropped, a reason with a number not in that finding or an instruction to authorise, pay,
approve, reject or hold is replaced by the deterministic reason, and forgotten findings keep their place.
The prompt carries claim numbers, amounts, dates and department names, never names or titles. Considered
model, cached 24 hours by the batch's contents, `AI_BATCH_CHECK_DAILY_LIMIT` (default 20) per person per
day spent only on a real model call, every call written to the audit log as `AI_BATCH_CHECK`, feedback as
`AI_FEEDBACK`. The model is called in one function, `batch_check_ai._ask_model`.
