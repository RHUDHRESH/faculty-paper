# Principal: the Reports hub (/reports/all)

## 1. Who and why
The Principal when she has been asked something and does not know which page answers it. "What's the actual job to be done by the Principal when she views the report?" (owner). Weekly around council season.

## 2. What it showed today
Screenshot: `shots/reports-hub-before.png`.

- Eight rows named for the system's pages: Analysis, Year brief, Build a report, Accreditation, Search, Publications, Journals, Past claims. "Analysis" and "Build a report" sound like the answer and are not; "Publications" is a list of 95 claims, not the college's publications.
- No figure and no answer. Nothing said whether the year went well or what was unready.
- Two columns of equal weight with no order; the page the council reads (Year brief) sits second.

## 3. What changes
- The hub is a list of her questions, each with today's answer as a sentence, opening the page that holds the proof:
  - Are we better than last year, and where?
  - Which departments need a push?
  - What goes in the council pack? (with the Council PDF beside it)
  - Where do we stand for NAAC and NIRF?
  - What did the scheme cost, against the budget?
  - Which papers make up a figure?
- The Answer strip on top: papers this year, per teacher, departments needing a push, things to settle before the council pack goes (each a link).
- Below, in lesser weight: "Look something up" (Search, Journals, Past claims, Claims by stage) and "Make your own" (Analysis, Build a report).
- Other roles keep the generic hub.
